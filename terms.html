Ordering

Placing an order request on this site does not create a binding contract and no payment is taken. After receiving your request we confirm availability and the total, including shipping, and send you a secure payment link. The contract is formed when you complete payment.

Prices and payment

Prices are shown in currency and include / do not include VAT (moms): confirm with Skatteverket or an accountant. Shipping is added by region and shown before you send your request. Payment is made by card / Swish / PayPal through payment provider, e.g. Stripe.

The works

Every print is printed by hand. Small variations in inking and paper between impressions of the same edition are part of the process and are not defects. Colours on screen may differ slightly from the work itself.

Editions are limited and numbered. Stock shown on the site is updated by hand; if a work sells out before your payment, we refund you in full.
