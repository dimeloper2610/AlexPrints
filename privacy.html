Who is responsible

Alex Fragoulis, address, email, is responsible for the personal data handled through this site.

What we collect

  • Order requests: name, email, phone (optional), shipping address and the works you chose. Used to answer your request, arrange payment and ship the order. Legal basis: taking steps towards a contract with you.
  • Contact form: name, email and your message, to reply to you. Legal basis: legitimate interest in answering enquiries.
  • Payments: handled by payment provider; we do not see or store your card details.

Order records are kept for as long as Swedish bookkeeping law requires confirm period. Other messages are deleted when they are no longer needed.

Your browser

The site uses no tracking or advertising cookies. Your cart, shipping region and display choices are stored in your own browser (local storage) and never sent to us until you submit a form.

Services we use

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site and may log visitors' IP addresses for security.
  • Google Fonts and jsDelivr deliver fonts and a script; your browser contacts their servers to load them.
  • Form service, e.g. Formspree receives the forms and forwards them by email.
  • Payment provider, e.g. Stripe processes payments.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, have it corrected or deleted, or object to how it is used, by writing to email. You can also complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY).
