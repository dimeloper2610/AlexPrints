Packing

Prints are packed flat between rigid boards, or rolled in a sturdy tube for large sheets. Framed works are wrapped and boxed. Confirm how Alex packs each type.

Shipping costs

Right of withdrawal

If you are a consumer in the EU you can withdraw from your purchase within 14 days of receiving it, without giving a reason. Write to email with your order details. Return the work in its original condition and packaging within 14 days of telling us. Who pays return shipping: state it here. We refund the amount paid, including the standard delivery cost, within 14 days of receiving the work back.

Commissioned or personalised works cannot be returned unless they arrive damaged.

Damaged in transit

If a work arrives damaged, photograph the packaging and the work and write to email within X days. We will arrange a replacement impression, if one is available, or a full refund.
